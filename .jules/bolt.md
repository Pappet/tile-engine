## 2024-05-29 - Short-circuit expensive condition evaluations
**Learning:** Evaluating dynamic simulation conditions (like iterating over vectors, neighbor checks) per tile per tick is extremely expensive. By moving the pseudo-random probability checks (`mix_hash`) to execute *before* conditions in the periodic and collision reaction systems, we can short-circuit the entire condition evaluation for reactions with low probabilities. `mix_hash` also needs to be marked `#[inline]` to avoid function call overhead across crates during this hot path.
**Action:** Always place cheap, fast-to-compute filters (like probability checks) before complex dynamic evaluations in inner simulation loops. Ensure critical small math functions used across crate boundaries are explicitly marked `#[inline]` for performance.
